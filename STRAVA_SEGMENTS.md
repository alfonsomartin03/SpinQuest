# Segment ideas for SpinQuest

Seventeen named stretches of the course, with the exact start and end points as they appear in the `.fit` file SpinQuest exports
(the course is placed over the Bermuda Triangle; see the README). Lengths, grades and climbing are from the course itself; the
time is what the game's physics gives a 75 kg rider (84 kg with the bike) holding 3.5 W/kg (262 W), as a sense of scale.

**A warning before you try them on Strava.** SpinQuest files are *Virtual Rides* placed in open ocean. As far as I know, Strava
only lets you create a segment from a real outdoor GPS activity on a mapped road, and does not give virtual rides segment
efforts, so these probably cannot be created there as they are. They work as a naming scheme and a leaderboard design, and for
any platform that does allow custom segments (or for a club's own results sheet), but check Strava's current rules first.

Which roads a rider covers depends on their route: the **loop** segments are only ridden on the main loop (take the flat road
at the foot of the climb and you miss the Col, the tunnel and the descent; take the link back and you rejoin before the lake), the
**flat road** segments only on Meadow Mile, and the **Death Road** segments only if they take that fork late in the lap.
